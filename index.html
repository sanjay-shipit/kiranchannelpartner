Property advice built on clarity, not pressure.

Kiran Channel Partner is a real estate advisory and channel partner in Hyderabad. We work with property developers to help homebuyers and investors find residential property that suits their needs, budget and plans.

FAQs

Questions buyers often ask

Straight answers on fees, approvals, site visits and loans. Can’t find what you need? Get in touch.

Do you charge buyers an advisory fee?

No. For direct developer projects, our advisory comes with zero brokerage and zero service charges.

Are the featured projects approved and RERA registered?

The featured layouts in Kompally and Shadnagar are approved by HMDA/DTCP and carry TS-RERA registration numbers provided by their developers. We share these details upfront, and we recommend that you independently verify original title deeds and survey numbers before making any financial commitment.

Does booking a site visit reserve a property?

No. Site visits are complimentary and do not reserve a property or lock a price. A property is allocated only once the official booking advance is paid directly to the developer.

Can you help me with a home loan?

Yes. We help you connect with banking partners and prepare your application. Loan eligibility, interest rates and final sanction are decided by the bank.

Are you the developer of these projects?

No. Kiran Channel Partner is an authorised channel partner, not the developer or owner of the listed projects. Prices, availability and offers are set by developers and may change without notice.

When can I reach your team?

We are available Monday to Sunday, 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM, by phone, WhatsApp or email.
